Cybersecurity Knowledge

Ethical Hacking

One of my greatest passions is cybersecurity, especially ethical hacking. I have studied this field deeply and I continue to learn every day, because cyber threats evolve constantly and professionals must stay updated to protect systems and information.

Ethical hacking is the practice of using hacking techniques in a legal and authorized way to identify vulnerabilities in systems before malicious attackers can exploit them. Ethical hackers, also known as white hat hackers, help companies improve their security by finding weaknesses and proposing solutions.

I have studied the main phases of ethical hacking: reconnaissance, scanning, gaining access, maintaining access, and covering tracks. I understand how attackers think and what tools they use, which allows me to design better defenses and to help organizations protect their assets.

I am familiar with tools such as Kali Linux, which is a specialized operating system for penetration testing. I use Nmap to scan networks and discover open ports and services, Wireshark to analyze network traffic, Metasploit to test vulnerabilities, and Burp Suite to analyze web applications.

I have also studied common vulnerabilities such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), cross-site request forgery (CSRF), and buffer overflow. I know how to identify them, how to exploit them in controlled environments, and how to fix them in real systems.

My goal as a cybersecurity professional is to help companies and people protect their data from cyber attacks. I believe that security is not only a technical issue but also an ethical and social responsibility. I want to use my knowledge to make the digital world safer for everyone. I am currently preparing for international certifications in cybersecurity, such as CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) and CompTIA Security+, to strengthen my skills and my professional profile.
